<h1>About 👋</h1>

Principal Fullstack Engineer with over 20 years of experience designing and delivering scalable, high-performance, production-grade systems. I operate with strong architectural ownership, bridging engineering excellence with product-driven decision making.

I work extensively with Node.js, TypeScript, NestJS, React, Next.js and PostgreSQL, building complex applications that integrate secure authentication, real-time systems, WebSockets, robust APIs and distributed data pipelines. My experience spans from greenfield products to restructuring and scaling legacy systems, always prioritizing reliability, security, performance and maintainability.

In recent years, my focus has shifted toward AI-native applications, where Artificial Intelligence is embedded at the core of the product architecture. I designed and built Bymax Fitness, a production AI-native platform, defining the full system architecture across frontend, backend, data, payments and infrastructure.

The platform leverages Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to reduce hallucinations, vector databases for semantic search, structured prompt orchestration, and AI Agents with memory for deterministic and explainable decision-making. I have hands-on experience integrating LLMs into real products, combining deterministic business rules with AI reasoning, async pipelines, queues and scalable workflows to support high concurrency.

I also designed complete product systems including subscription models (plans, trials and vouchers), global payments via Stripe, asset management using S3-compatible storage, Redis for caching and pub/sub, WebSockets for real-time features, CI/CD pipelines and cloud deployments.

I am recognized for delivering real systems and for maintaining a pragmatic approach to AI, cost, performance and user experience. I adapt quickly to new domains and technologies and use AI as a tool to accelerate development, improve quality and support architectural decisions, without compromising engineering standards.
